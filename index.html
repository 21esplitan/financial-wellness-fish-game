Swim Upstream
The Time Value of Money Challenge

Can you beat the current?

Two money scenarios appear. Pick the better one. Get it right and your fish swims upriver. Get it wrong and the current drags it back.

  • 30 seconds on the clock. Answer as many as you can.
  • +100 per right answer, with bonus points for streaks.
  • −50 per wrong answer.
  • Reach the top of the river for a +300 bonus.

Tip: on a keyboard, press A or B (or ← →).
